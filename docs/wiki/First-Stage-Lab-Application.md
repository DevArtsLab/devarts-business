## 6-12 Month Plan

> Over the next year, we plan to design and launch DevArts Lab, a digital development and creative solutions studio that helps small businesses and professionals build a strong online presence using modern web technologies and AI-powered tools. We will focus on validating the concept through pilot projects, collecting client feedback, refining service packages, and establishing a simple yet scalable business model.

**Character count:** ~420/600

## Skills/Knowledge Goals

> We hope to gain practical business knowledge on customer discovery, pricing strategy, and marketing for early-stage service businesses. We also want to strengthen our financial literacy, pitching skills, and network with mentors who can guide us in turning DevArts Lab into a sustainable business that supports both creative and technical clients.
